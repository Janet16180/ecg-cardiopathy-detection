# Label-first younger and all-age supervised cohort assembly

Protocol for a new data selection, committed before selection. This is not an experiment score or an
identical reconstruction of a historical cohort. Both selections contain proxy-positive and
proxy-negative training ECGs, with a ceiling of 25,000 each; labels and signal quality are unchanged.

## Admission and provenance

Assembly reads completed training-quality tables, not waveforms, and a label-free mechanical held-out
reference table. The recovered Challenge split must match SHA-256
`5ecd82154fe9ed574b9706d9b7efbe417a578197845cf2a6a48615ca54959cc8`.
Quality receipts must bind their CSVs and the unchanged `ecg_quality.py`. A resource profile is
insufficient. The reference receipt must declare complete mechanical recovery and the original split.
Every artifact is hashed before use and rechecked before writing a completion receipt.

PTB-XL labels come from the recovered, byte-verified `labeled_train.csv`; unknown labels remain
excluded. The current SCREEN-01 qualification table restricts PTB to folds 1–7. Folds 8–10 and any known
held-out patient remain excluded, even when they were training in an older manifest. Only admitted
PTB training rows' demographic/annotation evidence fields are parsed from raw metadata. Administrative
IDs and folds establish this filter before targets and annotation fields are loaded.

Challenge uses only `supervised_eligible` training rows from the recovery adapter: defined original
primary proxy, unchanged quality, original duplicate rules and exactly 5,000 samples. A longer-record
crop's record label cannot enter a supervised cohort. All records are screened against held-out record
IDs, known patient IDs, canonical-window hashes and full-signal hashes. SPH never enters training.
EchoNext's structural endpoint, MIMIC machine labels and CODE-15 machine labels do not enter this target.

Across admitted sources, identical canonical windows with conflicting targets or known sex values
are all removed. Otherwise one copy is retained deterministically, prioritizing documented annotation
evidence and then record ID. Patient IDs are preserved where known; no Challenge patient identity is
invented. This removes exact observed waveform copies; it cannot establish patient independence for
sources without patient IDs or remove every possible near duplicate.

## Label evidence before age

The versioned [policy](cohort-label-evidence-policy-2026-10-28.json) defines the ordering. These ranks
express available documentation, not empirical label error rates or a claim that a hospital is superior.

- Rank 0: Chapman/Ningbo's documented physician labeling, secondary validation and senior adjudication
  process, supported by the [release methods](https://physionet.org/content/ecg-arrhythmia/1.0.0/).
- Rank 1: PTB records with `validated_by_human=True`, and other admitted human-read Challenge sources.
  The [Challenge description](https://moody-challenge.physionet.org/2021/) states that reader counts and
  label quality vary. Do not assert per-record multireader evidence for CPSC/Georgia from that statement.
- Rank 2: PTB `initial_autogenerated_report=False` without human-validation evidence. This records
  physician-origin evidence, not a second-reader claim. Unvalidated automatic or unknown origins are
  excluded. The [PTB release](https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/) distinguishes initial report
  generation, human validation and technical waveform review.

## Two selections

Within each evidence rank, the younger arm selects ages 18–25 first, then adults older than 25 in
ascending age. Ties use the existing selector's seed-42 record-ID hash order. Minors and unknown ages
are excluded from this arm. Better-supported labels always precede age preference, so an older
rank-0 record can precede a younger rank-1 record. The all-age comparator applies the same label policy
and seed with no age priority; minors and unknown ages may enter and are flagged. There is no class,
source or sex quota and no automatic balancing. Both classes must actually be present in each output.

Preserve raw ages and their interpretation. PTB ages 300+ represent privacy-obscured ages above 89;
store a lower bound of 90 plus `age_right_censored=True`, never a literal age of 300 or a true age of 90.
Age summaries report these records separately and exclude them from exact-age means and medians.
Challenge age zero/nonpositive/invalid values are missing. Do not replace unknown age with youth.

Output counts may be below 25,000. Do not weaken label evidence, quality, split or duplicate rules to
fill a quota. Overlap between these two training cohorts is allowed and reported; overlap with
evaluation is excluded before selection. Source/class composition can change with age preference, so
the two manifests do not by themselves isolate a causal effect of age.

## Artifacts and execution

New output directory: admitted candidates, exclusion ledger, younger/all-age manifests, aggregate
summaries and a completion receipt with exact input/source/policy/output hashes. Report source/family,
binary target, label rank, known patients, exact-age distribution, censored/missing ages, duplicate and
held-out exclusions, and inter-cohort record/hash/patient overlap. No model is fitted or scored.

Run only after the parent confirms the recovery/reference gate. All patient-level artifacts remain
local. A missing input or failed gate raises; it cannot silently produce a PTB-only finished cohort.
