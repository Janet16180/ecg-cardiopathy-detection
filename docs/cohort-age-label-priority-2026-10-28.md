# New cohorts: label reliability before age preference

Design draft, not an executed cohort or model result. The user requested a younger cohort for the
Italian student-screening context and a comparison cohort covering the full age range, with cleaning
preserved and higher-quality labels taking precedence over youth. The working interpretation is two
cohorts of up to 25,000 ECGs, each containing both proxy-positive and proxy-negative ECGs.

## Shared eligibility

- Restore and verify the original split and deduplication artifacts first. New selections never redefine
  the protected groups. SPH remains outside training; EchoNext test remains closed.
- Reuse the frozen signal-quality policy and canonical waveform conversion. Review flags remain flags;
  only the existing exclusion rules remove records. Log every exclusion.
- Keep the existing ECG-annotation proxy label definition. Undefined findings do not become normal.
- For supervised training, require a defined compatible label from a human-read source. MIMIC and
  CODE-15 machine labels cannot fill a quota. EchoNext's echo-confirmed structural endpoint is a different
  target and cannot be silently substituted for the ECG-annotation proxy.
- Record known patient identities and source families; remove exact signal copies. Do not manufacture
  patient identities for sources that lack them.

## Selection priorities

First enforce data eligibility and signal quality. Then prioritize documented label reliability for
the same endpoint. Use per-record evidence of expert verification/adjudication where actually available;
do not assume that every label from one hospital is superior, or that missing verification means an
automatic label. If sources cannot be distinguished reliably, assign the same evidence tier and state
that limitation. Dataset reputation, model confidence and class rarity are not label-quality measures.

Within an equal label-quality tier, the younger cohort prioritizes the target university-age range
(working target 18–25, inclusive). Any expansion beyond that range must be explicit in the final
selection specification; age is not a diagnosis and pediatric records are not substitutes for students.
Unknown or invalid ages must be reported separately and never treated as young. A lower-quality label
does not outrank a higher-quality label merely because its patient is younger.

The comparison cohort uses the same eligibility and label priorities without favoring younger ages.
Use a fixed reproducible sampling seed within equal priorities. Final counts may be smaller than 25,000
if trustworthy eligible labels are insufficient; do not relax cleaning or relabel records to reach a quota.

Before selection, freeze the exact age expansion rule, reliability tiers and sampling constraints.
Report size, age distribution/missingness, sources/families, label evidence, positive/negative counts,
patient counts where known, duplicate removals and signal-quality exclusions for both cohorts. Overlap
between their training records is permissible and must be reported; overlap with evaluation is not.

These are new cohorts with new manifests and receipts. Restoring an old pipeline does not make an
age-prioritized selection identical to an old v3/v4 cohort. Differences in source and class composition
may accompany the age preference and must not be attributed solely to age.

## Label provenance evidence

The [PTB-XL release](https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/) describes both physician-generated
and automatically generated initial reports, with many subsequently validated by a cardiologist.
Preserve `initial_autogenerated_report` and `validated_by_human` as separate evidence fields;
technical signal review is not equivalent to validation of the diagnostic label. Do not promote
folds 9 or 10 into training merely because their annotation quality is higher.

The [Chapman/Ningbo release](https://physionet.org/content/ecg-arrhythmia/1.0.0/) describes physician
labeling, a second physician's validation and senior adjudication of disagreements. This is evidence
about the source's annotation process, not an independently measured error rate for every record.
Preserve that distinction in the new cohort's provenance fields.

## Reconstruction permission

The user explicitly approved reading Challenge labels and waveforms only to reconstruct the data:
"Sí, solo para reconstruir los datos." This covers deterministic cleaning, hashes, duplicate groups
and original split recovery. It does not authorize model fitting or scoring on reserved groups, final
evaluation, or reading EchoNext test. No patient-level outputs leave the local machine.
