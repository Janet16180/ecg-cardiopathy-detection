Keep the saved full-label JEPA and both CPC probes if their stored development AUROCs reproduce. Record the limited refit's selected C and development AUROC beside the original for transparency.

Check feature hashes against available metadata, probe file hashes in the new receipt, unique ECG IDs, patient ID and split agreement with each manifest, identical budget-specific labeled records, and disjoint train/development/calibration/test patients. No trainable stack or weight tuning beyond this grid.

## Development screen and gate

On development alone, assign patients to five fixed stratified group folds (seed 14042). For each fold, choose the highest inclusive raw-logit threshold attaining at least 95% sensitivity on the other four folds, then predict the held-out fold. Aggregate held-out specificity and attained sensitivity across all five folds. AUROC uses the full development scores, each a held-out prediction from the saved supervised probes. Save the fold assignment hash. Pick the alpha with the highest cross-fitted specificity, then AUROC, then closest to .5, then lower alpha. Also report all five arms. For a patient-cluster bootstrap (300 draws, seed 14042), reuse each sampled patient's fixed fold assignment and repeat the cross-fitted selection to assess weight stability. Bootstrap estimates are diagnostic and do not become the point estimate.

Advance a budget to calibration/test only if its selected alpha is interior, its development specificity exceeds the better endpoint by at least **0.02 absolute**, its AUROC is no more than **0.002** below the better endpoint, at least one adjacent interior weight also beats the better endpoint on specificity, at least half of bootstrap selections are interior, and at least 75% of bootstrap draws favor the point-selected weight over the better endpoint on specificity. These are prespecified practical screen rules, not significance criteria. A negative gate ends that budget's study without reading calibration or test labels/predictions in the runner.

If the gate passes, fit a new positive-slope Platt logistic calibrator on calibration patients only, select the inclusive 95%-sensitivity threshold there, and evaluate the fixed choice once on test. Use existing evaluation helpers and 500 patient-cluster test bootstrap draws. The runner may hash and verify test-manifest identity before the gate, but does not read test labels or compute test scores until after a positive gate. Do not use calibration or test to revise the blend or checkpoint. A positive screen can motivate a separately costed follow-up using a saved end-to-end CPC checkpoint; this study does not silently substitute that model.
