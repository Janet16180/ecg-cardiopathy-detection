# Experiment 016 v6: seed-43 one-epoch DropPath screen

The prespecified **Residual − Legacy** development AUROC contrast after 240 updates was **+0.03552** (2,000-draw paired whole-patient bootstrap 95% interval **+0.02646 to +0.04470**). Residual improved substantially over the historical DropPath rule under this one-epoch warmup, meeting the mechanistic point-estimate screen. It remained **0.01372 AUROC below the frozen clean probe**, so the result is a **partial mechanism explanation, not a practical rescue**. The Off control was similar to Residual; neither arm met the frozen probe-relative rescue threshold. Retain the frozen probe for this study and do not advance to calibration/test or more training automatically.

The secondary Off − Legacy AUROC was **+0.03510** (95% paired interval **+0.02531 to +0.04541**). Residual − Off was **+0.00042** (interval **−0.00657 to +0.00758**), giving no clear discrimination between correction and removal under this budget. All **2,000/2,000** patient-bootstrap draws were valid. Intervals condition on one model per arm and do not include training-seed uncertainty. The five threshold folds grouped patients and targeted 95% sensitivity using each fold's training portion. The development set contained **1,306 ECG records from 1,173 patients**; it was inspected in the earlier v5 profile, so this new optimization seed is not an independent cohort confirmation.

The [frozen v6 protocol](experiment-016-droppath-rescue-v6.md) used the exact clean 15,359-record full-label training cohort, released xECG and unchanged fixed-C probe. All three arms started from identical released/probe tensors and used optimization seed 43, 15,359 exposures, 240 updates, and the **first 240 multipliers of the original two-epoch schedule**; they stopped after one warmup epoch. The v5 initial mode diagnostic was inherited by hash: mean train/eval logit shift **7.03** for Legacy, **4.10** for Residual, and **0** for Off. The [v6 bridge](../outputs/experiment016_droppath_rescue_v6/bridge.json) passed seed-43 CPU checkpoint and same-device replay checks for every arm before training. The three measured passes took **542.40, 540.12, and 530.46 seconds**. The conservative final cost projection was **4,528.85 seconds**, below the **7,200-second** gate; fixed-set gradient audit plus report took **84.85 seconds**, within their 300-second allowance. The [final report](../outputs/experiment016_droppath_rescue_v6/report.json), [gradient audit](../outputs/experiment016_droppath_rescue_v6/gradient_audit.json), and hashed arm checkpoints/logits/histories are local.

The gradient audit measured the **evaluation-mode** gradient on the same fixed 128 training ECGs at initialization, after the zero-LR first update, and after update 240. Its norms and hypothetical 3.0 clipping eligibility are in each trajectory; one unused parameter tensor legitimately had no gradient. These fixed-set norms are distinct from the actual training-batch gradients. The runner recorded the first update's actual preclip norm, but the frozen update helper did not retain the final training-batch preclip norm. The [original full manifest](../outputs/experiment_queue_016_rescue_v6_full/queue.json) completed all three training arms then stopped at an audit-only `grad=None` handling error. Its source, log and checkpoints were preserved. A [new verified audit/report-only successor](../outputs/experiment_queue_016_rescue_v6_audit_v2/queue.json) corrected that check and completed without retraining. All arm artifacts, final audit/report receipts, and 61-entry successor source map passed post-run verification. The full manifest's `created_at` metadata was an inaccurate future planning timestamp; its status/log recorded the actual 09:58:15 UTC launch.

This is a one-seed, one-epoch screen on an ECG diagnostic annotation proxy. It does not establish converged fine-tuning behavior, independent reproducibility, clinical disease status, or referral suitability. Calibration and test outcomes remain untouched.
