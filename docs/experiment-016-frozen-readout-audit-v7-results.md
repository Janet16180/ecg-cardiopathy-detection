It therefore met the *near-complete practical recovery* rule, but **did not meet** the ≥0.002 potentially useful adaptation rule. Retain the released probe; no calibration/test or follow-up training was performed.

The prespecified secondary refit-minus-joint AUROC contrasts were **+0.01455** for Residual (paired interval +0.00913 to +0.02035) and **+0.04910** for Legacy (+0.03828 to +0.05997). Their refitted AUROCs were close to the released probe, but they do not change the Off-primary decision. The original released head on adapted features improved over each saved joint head by only +0.00120 to +0.00190 AUROC. All **2,000/2,000** whole-patient bootstrap draws were valid. The five threshold folds grouped the **1,306 development ECG records from 1,173 patients** and targeted 95% sensitivity using each fold's other four folds.

The [frozen protocol](experiment-016-frozen-readout-audit-v7.md) prohibited encoder updates and selected a train-only StandardScaler plus C=0.01 `lbfgs` logistic readout independently on each of the four frozen 1,024-wide backbones. The same clean **15,359 training ECG records** and development records were used throughout. A fresh released-control fit reproduced the saved logits to **1.78×10⁻¹⁴** maximum absolute difference and 0.9619404 AUROC. Off, Residual and Legacy fits converged in 96, 107 and 100 iterations; their saved joint-head logits reproduced the v6 predictions to at most **3.2×10⁻⁶**. All frozen encoder tensors remained unchanged, in evaluation mode and without gradients.

This is a postmortem readout audit on one seed-43, one-epoch adapted state per arm and a repeatedly inspected development cohort. Refit changes head optimization and regularization relative to joint AdamW; the result does not isolate head nonconvergence from encoder changes or prove no representation information was lost. Bootstrap intervals condition on fixed trained models and do not measure optimization-seed variation. The endpoint is an ECG diagnostic annotation proxy, not a clinical disease or referral outcome. Calibration and test remained untouched, and no further xECG rescue sweep was started.
