# Experiment 016 v8: matched-objective head optimization

The prespecified **standardized C minus raw B AUROC contrast at update 240** was **−0.00084** for head seed 44 and **−0.00060** for seed 45. Their arithmetic mean was **−0.00072** (2,000-draw paired whole-patient 95% interval **−0.00193 to +0.00051**). C had lower full-training probe-objective gaps than B at that endpoint, but neither seed met the required half-gap ratio, and neither showed the required +0.005 development gain. The frozen conditioning screen **failed**. A lower training objective without a development gain does not support the proposed short-budget discrimination benefit.

The prespecified stationary-feature AdamW control **A passed its separate sufficiency screen** at both seeds. At update 240, A reached **0.96249 / 0.96185 development AUROC** for seeds 44 / 45, gains of **+0.01469 / +0.01404** over the saved Off joint head at 0.94781, and within 0.002 of the v7 Off refit at 0.96293. B minus A at that same budget was essentially zero (**−0.000003 / −0.000026 AUROC**), so the regularization-package contribution screen failed. This shows that additional head-only AdamW fitting on fixed Off features sufficed under this diagnostic setup; it does **not** identify why the original joint encoder-and-head run ended with a poor head.

The full-training objective was the frozen `J = mean BCE + [1/(2 N C)]‖a‖²` with `N=15,359` and `C=0.01`. Its verified D reference was **0.1894311272343**: independent L-BFGS starts from the joint head and zero head differed by **1.05×10⁻¹³**, with standardized-gradient infinity norms **1.47×10⁻⁸ / 2.54×10⁻⁸**. At update 2,400, C minus B AUROC remained within 0.002 for both seeds, but neither arm's objective gap was within `1e-5` of D. Thus the frozen *B catches C* rule did not pass, and these finite-budget trajectories do not establish asymptotic behavior. A has no scalar objective equivalent to J: its displayed J gap is only a common diagnostic. At most two of 2,400 steps per arm were clipped, so clipping rarely affected these CPU head-only trajectories.
