# Research findings, 20 October 2026

Reference averaging makes the current ECG model's explanations more consistent, while the
explanations remain spread across many beats. We can now account exactly for its decision score
without inventing specialist labels. The four studies below were executed from committed protocols
and source, then independently reconstructed. All work remains local.

## What changed

| Study | Executed finding | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CLUSTER-06](clustering/cluster06-bank-scale/results.md) | Fixed regional clustering's lower-tail bank agreement rose from 0.5873 to 0.8416 with five-bank means. All 183 transformed covariance-floor controls reproduced raw fits. | Averaging helps this configuration, and numerical scale explains an earlier source of changing groups. A secondary record configuration worsened. |
| [CLUSTER-07](clustering/cluster07-regional-projection/results.md) | Regional variance retention rose from 10.79% to 14.41%, but native-versus-reference concordance stayed low: ARI 0.1532. | [NTT-08](localization/ntt08-contextual-contributions/results.md) | All 180 original score margins were reconstructed from signed contextual beat and token contributions within 1.01e-15. Contributions were diffuse and bank-sensitive. | We can explain the model's calculation and quantify its uncertainty. This does not supply clinical interval truth. |
| [NTT-09](localization/ntt09-reference-contribution-stability/results.md) | With each patient's finding contrast fixed, mean overlap of top-positive-quarter beat sets rose from 0.4418 to 0.6559. All 60 patients improved; every patient met coverage requirements. | Reference averaging improves conditional ranking consistency, with greater reference exposure and computation. |

The overlap score is Jaccard: shared selected beats divided by the union of selected beats. It
measures agreement between reference choices, not accuracy against a cardiologist. NTT-09 fixes
the same finding comparison for every bank, so its improvement is not explained by silently
changing which finding the explanation describes. Its effect was +0.2141, above the prospectively
declared +0.10 threshold.

## What the positive result does and does not establish

An individual bank uses three distinct support patients per finding. An ensemble uses fifteen
support slots, with repeats, covering six to nine distinct patients per finding, median eight.
The improvement therefore combines more reference exposure with averaging and normalization; an
equal-budget advantage has not been demonstrated. All references come from the same previously
explored finite pool. Bank pairs are dependent and are not independent statistical samples.

More consistent rankings also remained diffuse: NTT-09 median effective beat fraction rose from
0.9662 to 0.9854, while its top quarter carried only 32.94% of absolute contribution mass after
averaging. A hard selected region can conceal contributions elsewhere. Signed maps and variation
across references are better supported by these results than a single certain abnormality mark.
This is an interpretation of the present model behavior, not an expert-approved display rule.

QRS windows and V1/V6 are fixed pooling priors. The analysis explains the existing three-finding
reference classifier within that domain. Selection among P-wave, QRS, ST-segment and T-wave evidence
is not established. Each token retains full-record encoder context, so its time anchor does not
isolate the raw waveform that caused a prediction. Cached representation deletion tests the
downstream score, not a waveform counterfactual.

The cardiologist limitation remains explicit. No new independent clinical annotations exist, and
neither source diagnosis labels, stable clusters, SHAP values nor exact score contributions establish
disease causes or clinical evidence boundaries. Earlier NTT-06 and CLUSTER-03/05 primary failures
remain unchanged. No closed Challenge/EchoNext test or final frozen test was opened.

[integrated verification receipt](../../reports/verification/2026-10-20-autonomous.json) records both.

answers must retain their provenance and cannot become independent clinical ground truth. Both
questions require their own prospective work. No additional run is scheduled by this report.
