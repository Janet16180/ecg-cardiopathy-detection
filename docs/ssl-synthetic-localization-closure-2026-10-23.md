## What was established

The released generator was executed and locally saved ECG-shaped signals supported
controlled numerical interventions with known lead/time support. Small localizers
learned some of that introduced-change structure. A matched transfer study on real
backgrounds with an unfamiliar numerical editor passed its six fixed numerical
checks: mean direct AP0.032520, gains0.021489 over nuisance maps,0.010529 over an
amplitude baseline and0.019137 over donor masks. See the
[transfer report](ssl-synthetic-localization-background-editor-transfer-v1-results.md).
This is numerical transfer on introduced changes, not localization of disease.

Independent reconstructions, actual GPU profiles and reproducible matched fits
establish computational integrity. They do not supply the missing clinical truth.
Generated shape, visually plausible morphology, a known numerical intervention and
a disease-specific pathological region are different evidence levels.

## Why the stream is closed

No independent pathology-region reference exists among this stream's locally
qualified artifacts for its intended real12lead abnormality-localization endpoint.
The last experiments could test introduced edits and timing, but could not establish
what a cardiologist would judge suspicious from an independent pathology reference.
Further iterations would not remove that ground-truth wall. The user has closed the
direction; this document proposes no continuation. This is not a claim that no public
ECG annotations exist anywhere.
