# Independent factorial verification policy

18 October 2026. This policy fixes the verification scope before an independent audit run. It does
not change the original factorial comparison, forward evaluation correction or twelve decision
gates. No additional model fit, inference, threshold or patient selection is admitted.

Use independently written numerical code to reproduce the four saved probability banks against
the new support/candidate masks, all case AP/tied-area/response metrics and T/U/O reductions, all
four calibration thresholds and slice identities, patient aggregation, primary and prespecified
secondary paired intervals, control flags and all twelve gates. Reuse the shared whole-patient
resampling helpers, with the frozen seed/draws, but do not call production factorial metric or
decision calculators. Compare scientific numbers at absolute tolerance1e-12; report every mismatch
and exact case/patient denominators. Accounting comparisons allow1e-9 seconds and derive costs from
SHA-bound receipts rather than rounded report text.

Independently reconstruct the schedule, masks' actual scheduled class weight and exposures; inspect
CPU model state dictionaries for initial/head/encoder/final identities, exact predecessor BCE final
tensors and all600 losses, finite paired losses and component arithmetic, checkpoint digests and
source-bound recovery/execution records. Do not claim independently rerun optimizer updates or
unsaved optimizer states. Check source/input identity before and after calculations. Bind exact
preparation, profile, training, forward evaluation and parent audit receipts. Missing/conflicting
authority stops verification.

Numerical array access is limited to the four new probability banks, two new boolean masks,
and ONLY rows0–1999 of the predecessor support mask for native TRAIN class-weight reconstruction.
Do not access its old validation rows. The600×16 integer schedule is permitted execution metadata.
Original/predecessor/final model-only checkpoints and recovery model/optimizer/RNG checkpoints may
be read on CPU solely for state integrity; no CUDA or inference. No raw or processed ECG waveform payload is read or hashed.
Background-file and unused ancestor-payload identities depend on explicitly SHA-anchored passed
qualification/audit declarations; record every deferred exception and its authority. Mask generation
and raw-voltage diagnostics remain qualification/source-bound claims, not independent waveform
regeneration. No native clinical label or closed set is accessed.

Freeze the exact independent script/source hash, integrate/review it, and obtain coordinator GO
before ONE run. Keep an exact local script copy with the audit output. Run with CUDA hidden and one
numerical/PyTorch thread. CPU AND monotonic wall allowance are240seconds, with a two-second failure
receipt reserve; also enforce original1800-second new-stage CPU,14400 cumulativeCPU and4GiB whole
stream limits. All audit outputs/failures share the original64MiB study reserve. Refuse overwrite,
retain stopped attempts with exact costs, and recheck actual bytes before final receipt. Charge
verification once to the exact evaluation cumulative and stage costs; GPU-wall stays unchanged.
No retry or repair of a mismatch is automatic. Finish with a passed/failed verification report and
preserve earlier failed study decisions. All artifacts remain local without pushes or uploads.
