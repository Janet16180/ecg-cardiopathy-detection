## Scope and consequence

The result supports a measured tradeoff in this fixed numerical localization
study: mixed real backgrounds lower uncalibrated real control scores and do not
meet the requested held-out-editor AP gain; generated-domain preservation also
fails. It does not establish a general benefit of adding real data or justify
choosing a favorable fit/editor after seeing these outcomes. Both development
panels were previously spent and the bootstrap conditions on three fixed fits
and fixed donor assignments, not a population of future training runs.

The cohort proves the declared PTB source-local metadata-role/fold separation.
Absent historical v3/v4 and partition payloads remain absent; cross-hospital
biological identity, missing waveform-duplicate catalogs and the released GAN's
training membership remain unproven. Native findings were never labeled healthy
or diseased. Numerical masks identify only introduced changes; physiological
validity, disease control and real-disease localization remain unvalidated.
All data, individual identities, checkpoints and predictions remain local.
